# Montages: probe positions in, montages and shared groups out, and what a request names

wl.works records where each probe went and when. This piece takes that record (`PUT /positions`), derives each
session's montages and the electrodes montages share, lists them in `GET /sessions`, and holds a request to a
montage or group it listed. A request names one in `selection.montage_id`, or `selection.group_id` with the new
`shared` role; `metadata.montage_boundaries` is retired. A file's probes take the insertion their montage places
them at. wl.works pins at one merge of ours.
- **Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-montages-design.md` (`00c617d`).
- **Plan:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-24-montages.md` (`ae9cbaa`), twelve tasks, decisions D1 to D18.

  every refusal names the field and the `insertion_number`. `positions_request.json` exported.
- **Task 2, placing an edge** (`16ffeb7`, `e3937a8`). `montage/edges.py`: wl.works' `place()` kept exactly, with a

- **Task 5, `PUT /positions`** (`b4cecde`). The highest seq wins until the session's first accepted request; the
  statuses `404`, `409` (with `positions_seq`) and `422`.
- **Task 6, the daemon's stage** (`13d8670`, `01c4e84`). `montage/stage.py` derives each listable session and
  `montage/stored.py` stores it in new `core` tables, until the session's first accepted request. The derivation's
  row is the session's lock.
- **Task 7, the listing** (`b0f32e1`). `montages`, `shared_groups`, `positions_status`, `positions_seq`, and the
  flags `position_edge_ambiguous`, `position_edge_inside_run` and `probe_without_position`.
  `session_listing.json` regenerated.
- **Task 8, a request names a listed montage** (`672b58f`, `7de62b8`, `0453d3d`). `montage_boundaries` retired
  (`job_request.json` regenerated); the checks of the probes and runs against the stored montage (D14); positions
  changed since the montages were derived is a 422; the race with a re-derivation closed under the derivation's
  lock; a seq recorded alone after the first request is the derivation's too.
- **Task 9, the `shared` role** (`7334359`, `268fc8e`). `selection.group_id`, `SharedActivation`, `submit_shared`;
  one activation per group, `activation_id` 0, stored to wait for sorting.
- **Task 10, joining files to insertions** (`8494973`, `f6e9944`). Each SpikeGLX file joins every insertion whose
  placed position covers its time; a position whose serial the census never names takes its link back.
- **Task 11, the NWB build** (`23132e6`, `0c9a9fa`). A file's probes take their insertion and site map from the
  stored montage; a canonical's description names its shared groups; the notes for two site maps and for a probe
  with no sortable run.
- **Task 12, the records.** `docs/ops/lab-host-protocol.md` (`PUT /positions`, `GET /sessions`, `POST /jobs` with
  the `shared` role and its refusals, the NWB notes, the 404, 409 and 422 rows),

  `test_the_shared_role_and_the_retired_boundaries_are_documented`.

## Deploy

- **Development databases are rebuilt, never migrated.** New: in `core`, `MontageDerivation` (with its `Flag`),
  `MontageProbe`, `MontageSortableRun`, `PlacedPosition` and `SharedGroup` (with `Member` and `SortableRun`), beside
  the existing `Montage`; in `request`, `PositionSet` (with `Position`) and `SharedActivation` (with `ProbeRun`).
- **MySQL's `binlog_format`.** `montage/stored.py::write` and `responder/positions.py::put_positions` read in
  `READ COMMITTED`, which needs `binlog_format` `ROW` or `MIXED` where binary logging is on. MySQL 8 defaults to
  `ROW`. **Check serv.**
- **Every listed session is listed once more**, since each entry gains `montages`, `shared_groups`,
  `positions_status` and `positions_seq`. A session lists `waiting` until the daemon has passed it, and a session
  with ephys has no montage to request until wl.works has sent its positions.
- **`wl.yaml`:** no dependency changed.
